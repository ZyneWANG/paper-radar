原文摘要 · Abstract
While generative AI models can produce visually faithful artwork, they often fall short in conveying emotional authenticity--a key driver of human expression. In visual storytelling, particularly comic storyboarding, this gap becomes pronounced: effective storyboards require both technical knowledge (e.g., anatomical accuracy or scenic composition) and emotional insight (from lived experience). We explore how human-AI collaboration can support emotion-driven creativity, where the user's feelings guide generation and emotional resonance is the goal. In a controlled study with N=25 participants, we find that AI assistance significantly improves emotional expression, aesthetic quality, and exploration, but reduces users' creative ownership. Our findings offer broader insights for human-AI co-creation in the domain of comic storytelling, emphasizing balance between output quality and user freedom, and raising new challenges for image generation models in this domain.

综述AI在文化遗产记录、保存、阐释和公众参与中的应用，涵盖机器学习、计算机视觉、自然语言处理、生成式AI和3D技术。指出AI能增强数字化和分类，但也带来本真性、偏见和文化同质化等伦理风险。
原文摘要 · Abstract
Artificial intelligence (AI) is increasingly transforming the documentation, preservation, interpretation, and public engagement of cultural heritage. This review examines the opportunities, risks, and ethical challenges associated with the application of AI in cultural heritage management and research. Drawing on interdisciplinary literature spanning heritage studies, computer science, digital humanities, museum studies, and information ethics, the review synthesizes evidence on the use of machine learning, computer vision, natural language processing, generative AI, and three-dimensional technologies in heritage contexts. The findings indicate that AI can enhance the digitization and classification of cultural collections, reconstruct damaged or lost heritage assets, support archaeological analysis, improve multilingual access to heritage resources, and create more interactive experiences for museums and heritage sites. AI also offers opportunities for predictive conservation through automated monitoring of environmental conditions, material deterioration, and structural changes. However, these benefits are accompanied by significant risks, including algorithmic bias, inaccurate reconstruction, cultural misrepresentation, intellectual-property disputes, data privacy concerns, technological dependency, and the marginalization of communities whose cultural knowledge is incorporated into AI systems without meaningful participation or consent. Generative AI presents additional challenges because synthetic representations may blur distinctions between historically documented evidence and machine-generated interpretations. The review therefore emphasizes the importance of human oversight, transparent documentation of AI-generated outputs, culturally sensitive data governance, community participation, provenance tracking, and equitable access to digital heritage technologies. Overall, AI should be understood not simply as a technological tool but as a socio-cultural intervention whose deployment requires collaboration among technologists, heritage professionals, researchers, governments, and source communities. Responsible and inclusive AI governance can enable technological innovation while protecting the authenticity, diversity, ownership, and cultural significance of heritage resources.

中文导读
该论文提出PAIR，一个实时多模态智能体，通过评估脚手架推断情感状态并提供调节指导，结合语音、颜色和头像线索。在14天部署中，19名参与者使用了1093次会话，显示情感支持的有效性。
原文摘要 · Abstract
Sustained emotional support requires generative agents to connect momentary emotion inference and regulation with continuity across encounters. We present PAIR (Perceptual Affective Inference and Regulation), a real-time multimodal agent that reconstructs how an event is appraised into an emotional state. Appraisal scaffolds produce a valence-arousal-dominance estimate and select regulation guidance, delivered through conversation with coordinated speech, color, and avatar cues. Rolling memory carries context across sessions, and the scaffold re-runs after guidance. In a 14-day deployment with 19 participants, 1,093 sessions paired initial and post-guidance estimates with unanchored self-reports. Initial valence reached MAE 1.20 on the 9-point SAM scale (r=.68), dominance reached MAE 1.30, and arousal showed weak agreement even after coarsening. Self-reported emotional change varied with initial state, with the largest valence increases in sessions that began at negative valence. Perceived understanding was associated with greater valence increase and showed little correspondence with numerical prediction error. Over two weeks, helpfulness increased while input shortened; interviews traced personalization and companionship to relevant recall, context updates, and familiar dialogue. These findings connect inference accuracy to conversational and temporal patterns of support through per-event, first-person evaluation.

中文导读
该论文提出Weave Forcing，一种无需训练的框架，用于交互式长视频生成中的组合记忆重用。通过LLM进行语义槽路由，分解用户提示为角色和背景，实现跨镜头组合。
原文摘要 · Abstract
Recent advances in autoregressive video generation have improved temporal consistency over extended durations, yet interactive storytelling requires more than continuous scene extension: a new shot may combine characters and backgrounds from different historical shots. Whole prompt retrieval can overlook the distinct reference needs of individual components, while directly combining all historical memories may introduce unrelated visual content. To address these problems, we present Weave Forcing, a training-free framework for compositional memory reuse in interactive long video generation. First, we use an LLM for semantic slot routing to decompose user prompts into character and background descriptions and explicitly select suitable historical references for each component. To isolate the required content, masked memory weaving uses contrasting attention maps conditioned on semantic slots to construct refined semantic masks, selectively exposing relevant tokens from compressed historical KV memories to guide the generation of the current shot. We further introduce coverage adaptive RoPE to adjust temporal offsets and memory retention according to no, partial, or full reference coverage, addressing visual artifacts observed when incomplete historical references are positioned close to the current generation. Extensive experiments demonstrate that Weave Forcing improves cross-shot subject and background consistency while maintaining competitive visual quality and text alignment.

中文导读
提出AdvRole框架，通过对抗性上下文重写将角色扮演强化学习变成闭环课程：一个Actor学习角色扮演，一个Rewriter编辑角色档案和对话上下文，生成针对Actor的困难场景。实验表明该方法能持续提升角色扮演智能体的表现。
原文摘要 · Abstract
Role-playing agents based on large language models have been widely applied in areas such as personalized assistance and social simulation. Recent RL methods typically train on a fixed scenario pool collected before learning begins. This creates a distributional bottleneck: as the agent improves, the scenarios where it performs poorly also change, while the training distribution remains static. Therefore, we propose AdvRole, an adversarial context rewriting framework that turns role-playing RL into a closed-loop curriculum. AdvRole alternates between an Actor that learns to role-play and a Rewriter that edits character profiles and dialogue contexts into actor-specific hard scenarios. The Rewriter is trained with a performance-gap reward, which favors rewrites that reduce the current Actor's score relative to the original scenario. As a result, the scenario pool evolves with the Actor and continuously targets under-mastered regions of the character-context space. Experiments on three role-playing benchmarks covering English and Chinese, as well as a new multilingual benchmark we release, show that AdvRole consistently outperforms baselines.

原文摘要 · Abstract
This study investigates the potential of a Virtual Reality immersive environment to support local heritage learning in primary education. A virtual museum of Badajoz was developed using Delightex and structures into three themed rooms: History, Monuments, and Historical Figures. The environment combined 360° images, narrated explanations, and gamified activities to create an interactive and immersive learning experience. The intervention was implemented with 105 students from 4 th , 5 th , and 6 th grade in two public schools. A quasi-experimental design was employed to assess knowledge acquisition through pre- and post-tests, together with a Likert-type questionnaire examining students’ perceptions of the experience. The results showed a substantial improvement in students’ knowledge of local heritage after the intervention, along with highly positive evaluations in terms of engagement, perceived usefulness, and willingness to repeat the experience. These findings suggest that Virtual Reality can be an effective and feasible tool for designing immersive, motivating, and context sensitive learning experiences in primary education.

原文摘要 · Abstract
Creating believable vh requires the coherent integration of perception, reasoning, and action mediated by language. A central challenge is to combine these components into a control loop grounded in interactive 3D environments. To this end, we present A.D.A.M.O. (Agent for language-Driven Actions with Multimodal Observations), a visual-symbolic framework for language-driven vh that leverages a pretrained vlm with tool calling to unify perception, reasoning, and action within a single control loop. A.D.A.M.O. maintains a dual visual-symbolic world model that combines egocentric visual input and synchronized symbolic state to support grounded task-oriented behavior from natural language prompts. To support diagnostic evaluation, we introduce a controlled task suite organized by a cd taxonomy that breaks down spatial tasks into procedural and linguistic complexity. Experiments in controlled scenes show that semantic labeling strongly influences task completion and failure modes, reducing perceptual ambiguity while shifting failures toward downstream execution, whereas reasoning errors remain comparatively rare.
